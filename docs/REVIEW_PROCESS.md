# Review Process

Review university data changes for:

- source license compatibility,
- public-only data,
- stable IDs,
- duplicate institutions,
- consistent Arabic and English names,
- source attribution,
- validation output.

For programs, check the university, faculty or institute, and campus against
the specific admissions row and the source that confirms the qualification.
Check the academic year for admission routes and confirm the discipline
classification separately from the award level.
See the [program pilot](post-seed/PROGRAM_PILOT_2026_2027.md) and the
[named program review](post-seed/NAMED_PROGRAM_REVIEW_2026_2027.md)
for an example of recording admission routes at the level the source supports.
For city-grouped rows, see the [public affiliation review](post-seed/PUBLIC_CITY_PROGRAMS_2026_2027.md)
for examples of corroborating the awarding university and teaching site.
Check announcement 5 before assigning an institute: health, tourism, Sharia
and several technical institutes belong to other ministries. When an award
comes from an archived copy of a university page, check the snapshot date
and prefer a current page when one exists.
The [bulk named-program review](post-seed/BULK_NAMED_PROGRAMS_2026_2027.md)
shows how to cross-reference institution and campus matches with award evidence.

For image assets, also confirm the licence permits reuse, the creator and
licence are kept for attribution, and the image depicts the target institution.
Cover photos must come from free-licence Wikimedia Commons files.

For post-seed additions, also confirm that candidates are present in the internal
approved production scope, in dataset scope, and not duplicates or aliases of
existing records. Candidates found only in public secondary sources should stay
in the post-seed backlog until there is an explicit scope decision.
