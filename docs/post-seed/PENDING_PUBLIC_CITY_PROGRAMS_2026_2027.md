# Pending city-grouped 2026–2027 offerings

The [third city-grouped review](PUBLIC_CITY_PROGRAMS_2026_2027.md#third-review-university-assignments)
assigns 177 offerings from the 1,031 city-grouped candidates, and the
[fourth review](PUBLIC_CITY_PROGRAMS_2026_2027.md#fourth-review-award-from-the-table-type)
records 168 of the 206 leads that needed only the award. This register
lists the rest: 262 leads. Each row is one offering lead: a city group,
campus and field of study, with quota variants, science variants and
repeated applicant tables joined. The university column is the candidate
the city group points to, not a verified assignment.

- **Award** leads were the ones the register treated as having a sourced
  university and teaching city. Under the owner decision of 13 November
  2026 the table type gives the award once an approved source gives the
  university and city, so the fourth review recorded 168 of them. The
  38 left need what the last column names:
  - an approved source for the Ain al-Arab (14), Afrin (1) or Qamishli
    (18) faculty. Decree 250 article 2 names no faculty there, and the
    Council decisions of 18 October 2026 the register relied on are not
    approved source records;
  - the teaching campus of the legal and administrative institute: its
    row has the Aleppo campus, but its label and announcement 5 name
    Ain al-Arab;
  - a Syrian-applicant row at the town campus, for four leads that appear
    only in the non-Syrian and Arab and foreign applicant tables
    (announcements 8 and 9): Arabic and Primary Education in Manbij and
    the two Sharia specializations in Al-Dana. Their Syrian-applicant
    rows give the Aleppo or Idlib campus and name the town in the label.
- **Campus and award** also needs a source that places the program at the
  Ministry campus, for example a branch faculty in Manbij, Qamishli,
  As-Suwayda or Quneitra.
- **Affiliation, campus and award** needs all three: no official source yet
  names the university for that faculty or institute in that city.
- **Other provider** rows belong to the ministry that announcement 5 names.
  They are not university offerings and stay out of the catalogue.

Locators use the first city-grouped row of each lead. Rows for the same
lead in other tables (announcements 8 and 9) share its status.
