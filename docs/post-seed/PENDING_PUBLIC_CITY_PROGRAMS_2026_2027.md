- **Award** needs a university page, regulation, study plan or Gazette
  decision that names the degree for that program. The university and the
  teaching city are already sourced.
- **Campus and award** also needs a source that places the program at the
  Ministry campus, for example a branch faculty in Manbij, Qamishli,
  As-Suwayda or Quneitra.
- **Affiliation, campus and award** needs all three: no official source yet
  names the university for that faculty or institute in that city.
- **Other provider** rows belong to the ministry that announcement 5 names.
  They are not university offerings and stay out of the catalogue.

Locators use the first city-grouped row of each lead. Rows for the same
lead in other tables (announcements 8 and 9) share its status.
