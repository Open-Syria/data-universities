# November 2026 field-of-study offering review

Reviewed on 16 November 2026 against the [Ministry admissions notices for
2026–2027](https://mohe.gov.sy/ar/media-center/details/651), later Ministry
decisions, and university sources. The reviewed records cover 11 of the 20
fields. The release adds 14 offerings and corrects the field on one existing
offering. Each programme preserves the qualification and teaching location
established by its sources. The import [manifest](../../imports/manifests/mohe-field-offerings-2026-2027-2026-11-16.json)
records the scope.
