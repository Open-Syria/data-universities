# Pending named 2026–2027 offerings

The pinned OpenSyria admissions crosswalk contains 491 named candidates.
The named review matched 356 of them to 343 catalogue offerings. Batch 3
resolves 38 more and the fourth city-grouped review 21 (below), so 415
candidates now match catalogue offerings. This page records the other 76
candidates. Counts are admissions candidates, so spelling and
applicant-route variants may point to the same offering. The Damascus
business institute admission row supports four separately verified awards.

| Evidence needed | Candidates |
| --- | ---: |
| Award | 55 |
| Campus | 15 |
| Campus and award | 2 |
| Program and award | 4 |

- **Award** requires a program, institute, or qualification source for
  the specific campus. An institute name or study duration alone does
  not establish the credential.
- **Campus** requires confirmation that the named program is taught at
  the listed site during this intake. The Syrian University for Science
  and Technology lists a permanent site in Maarrat al-Nu'man and an
  interim site in Azaz. Its timeline gives early 2027 for the move.
  Cordoba's Qamishli branch also needs program-level confirmation.
- **Campus and award** requires both. Decision 186 opens Bilad al-Sham's
  English Dawah specialization without naming the awarding complex or
  the credential. Al-Sham Private University's separate Tourism
  intake needs the opening decision or equivalent program evidence
  for Al-Tall.
- **Program and award** requires confirmation of the named faculty
  offering. Al-Shamal lists anesthesia and laboratory analysis as
  institutes on its program pages. The Ministry names faculty rows as
  well, so the two paths need independent confirmation.
- **Professional degrees** (entry-to-practice Medicine and Dentistry) are
  no longer pending on the award. On 13 November 2026 the owner decided to
  record them at the bachelor level as Human Medicine (الطب البشري) and
  Dentistry (طب الأسنان), and accepted Higher Education Council Decision 31,
  article 49, as award evidence when the Ministry row names the university
  and campus. Decision 31 covers only the award: the Dentistry row at the
  Syrian University for Science and Technology stays under **Campus**,
  because its Maarrat al-Nu'man site is unconfirmed and its domain no
  longer resolves.
