# Standalone technical institutes in the 2026–2027 catalogue

This scope extension adds 11 ministry-operated technical institutes and 23
named technical diploma tracks across six fields. The new provider records
carry their own bilingual names and verified locations. Technical tracks are
linked to the institute that delivers them. The original 57 institutions
retain their logo coverage requirement. A verified standalone institution
can be published with a text-only profile.

## Evidence and programme crosswalk

The [2026–2027 Ministry notice bundle](https://mohe.gov.sy/ar/media-center/details/651)
establishes current intake, authority, and teaching locality. Notice 5 lists
the specializations available at each institute. Notices 2 and 4 confirm the
institute-level admissions choices. A track can form part of an institute's
intake without a separate general or parallel admissions row. The catalogue
leaves routes empty for these tracks. The independent sources below
corroborate provider facts or the qualification, and do not supply current
admissions or admission conditions.
