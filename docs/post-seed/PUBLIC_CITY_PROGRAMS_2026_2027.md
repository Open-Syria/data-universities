# 2026–2027 public programs grouped by city

The pinned [admissions catalogues](https://github.com/Open-Syria/website/tree/07545062e6a0f06fde4540a7f1dc460ae2f7932e/src/app/%5Blocale%5D/admissions/_data/2026-2027)
contain 4,303 city-grouped choices. Collapsing repeated applicant routes
by city-group ID, campus, governorate, Arabic label, and kind gives 1,031
review candidates. Arabic labels use Unicode normalization and collapse
whitespace, diacritics, and tatweel. Different labels can still refer to
the same award. Record one offering per verified university, award, and
teaching campus. See the [affiliation review](ADMISSIONS_2026_2027_REVIEW.md#public-affiliation-and-next-import).

## Third review: university assignments

Batch 3 of the program plan assigns city-grouped rows to universities.
The 1,031 candidates collapse into 620 offering leads per city group,
campus and field of study: quota variants, the مجموع and تخصصي science
variants and repeated applicant tables join one lead. The pending rows are
in the [city-grouped register](PENDING_PUBLIC_CITY_PROGRAMS_2026_2027.md).

An offering is recorded only when official sources give all three facts:

- **University.** The university's own faculty or institute page, an
  establishment decree, article 2 of the University Organization Law's
  executive regulations (Decree 250 of 2006, as amended), a Higher
  Education Council decision, or announcement 5, which names the body that
  runs each applied college and technical institute.
- **Teaching campus.** The same sources, placing the faculty in the
  Ministry row's city.
- **Award.** The university's faculty page, internal regulation, study
  plan or guide, or the Official Gazette, naming the degree for that
  program. A faculty statement applies to the departments it lists.

University pages were read on 13 November 2026. Where a current page names
no award, an archived copy of the university's own page in the Internet
Archive is cited by its snapshot URL. Al-Baath and Tishreen sources apply
to Homs University and the University of Latakia under Decrees 99 and 100
of 2025. Directories and third-party profiles are not used in this batch.
The 177 offerings use 75 fields of study; none is left without one.

### Homs University

The university published the degrees of each faculty and institute on
4 October 2026: the medical sciences, engineering, humanities, economics
and sciences faculties, and the technical institutes. Faculty sites
confirm Medicine, Pharmacy, Architecture, Informatics and Music Education.
The faculty and institute overviews place each unit in Homs.

- The Faculty of Economics has one first degree, in Business
  Administration. The offering uses that field of study, not Economics.
- The Artificial Intelligence track of Informatics Engineering has its own
  field of study, as the field-of-study decisions require.
- The Badia Affairs and Desertification institute is listed under Palmyra,
  and announcement 5 says it teaches in Homs. Its award is an Agricultural
  Sciences technical diploma in that specialty, separate from the
  agricultural institute's diploma.
- The new Faculty of Sharia opens in 2026–2027 and has no published award
  yet.
